Privacy

Glowfic Transcript — browser extension and bookmarklet.

Glowfic Transcript reads a thread you are looking at on glowfic.com and turns it into a markdown or HTML file. Everything it does happens in your browser.

What it reads

When you click the button on a thread, it requests that thread's text from glowfic.com's own API, using the login you already have in that browser. It reads only the thread you asked for, and only when you ask. It has no access to any other site.

Where that goes

Nowhere. The text is turned into a transcript and handed straight back to you to copy or save. In particular:

  • Nothing is sent to the developer, or to any third party.
  • There is no server. There is no account. There is nothing to log in to.
  • There is no analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting of any kind.
  • Nothing is stored beyond the browser tab — closing it discards everything.
  • Your glowfic password is never asked for and never seen.

What you do with the file

Once you copy or download a transcript, what happens to it is up to you. If you paste it into another application — a chat assistant, a notes app, an e-reader — that application's own terms and privacy policy apply to it. This tool has no involvement in that step and no way to know about it.

Permissions

The extension requests two permissions and no standing access to any website: activeTab, which grants access to a single tab at the moment you click the toolbar button, and scripting, which is what lets it put the export panel into that tab. It holds no permission when you are not using it.

Other people's writing

A glowfic thread is written by its authors. This tool exports what you are already logged in and permitted to read, for your own use. It does not bypass any access restriction, and locked threads you cannot read stay unreadable. Please don't republish or redistribute other people's work with it.

Contact

The source is public at github.com/jacobfgrant/glowfic-transcript; questions and problems are best raised there as an issue.
